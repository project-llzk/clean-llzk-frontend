Note the one outlier above. The *generic* theorem `byteTable_certifies` holds for
any prime p and needs only the three standard axioms. Its instantiation at
`pBabybear` -- `Examples.byteTable_certified` -- additionally picks up
`primeBabybear` and `Fact (pBabybear > 512)`, which Clean proves by
`native_decide`. That is a pre-existing property of Clean/Utils/Primes.lean,
untouched by this work, and it is inherited by every proof about a concrete
Clean prime field. Recorded because an axiom list is only worth reading if the
surprises in it are explained.
