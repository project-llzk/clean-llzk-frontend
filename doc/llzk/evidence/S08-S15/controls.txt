## Canonicity, pinned alongside the controls

The comparison must not report a mismatch for two constraints that are equal as
polynomials but written differently, or every green above would be luck.

  x*y          vs y*x                          equal
  x+y          vs y+x                          equal
  x*(y*z)      vs (x*y)*z                      equal
  (x+y)*z      vs x*z + y*z                    equal
  x + (-1)*x   vs 0                            equal
  x*y          vs x*z                          NOT equal
  const 7      vs const 8                      NOT equal

The last two matter: without them the first five would be satisfied by a normal
form that collapses everything to the same value.
