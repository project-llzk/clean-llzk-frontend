## Control 3 (NEW) — corrupt @constrain only, leave @compute intact

Multiply's constraint is `w0 - x*y = 0`, emitted with the coefficient
`felt.const 2013265920` (= -1). Replacing it with `felt.const 7` inside
@constrain only turns the constraint into `w0 + 7*x*y = 0`, a different and
wrong constraint system.

## Control 4 (NEW) — delete the whole body of @constrain

Addition8FullCarry with @constrain reduced to `function.return` — no lookup,
